### Problem Statement

A company is considering two alternatives for a new project. Alternative A has an estimated cost of $150,000 and is expected to generate $200,000 in revenue. Alternative B has an estimated cost of $120,000 with expected revenue of $180,000. Calculate the net financial outcome for each alternative.

**Given:**
- Cost of Alternative A: $150,000
- Revenue from Alternative A: $200,000
- Cost of Alternative B: $120,000
- Revenue from Alternative B: $180,000

**Find:**
- Net financial outcome for Alternative A
- Net financial outcome for Alternative B

### Solution

### 1. Approach & Strategy
- Calculate the net financial outcome for each alternative by subtracting the estimated cost from the expected revenue.
- Compare the results to determine which alternative is financially more favorable.

### 2. Step-by-step Calculations
#### Step 1: Calculate Net Financial Outcome for Alternative A
- The net financial outcome is calculated using the formula:
$$
\text{Net Outcome} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Cost}
$$
- **Given values:**
  - **Cost of Alternative A:** $150,000
  - **Revenue from Alternative A:** $200,000

$$
\text{Net Outcome A} = 200,000 - 150,000
$$

#### Step 2: Calculate Net Financial Outcome for Alternative B
- Using the same formula for Alternative B:
$$
\text{Net Outcome} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Cost}
$$
- **Given values:**
  - **Cost of Alternative B:** $120,000
  - **Revenue from Alternative B:** $180,000

$$
\text{Net Outcome B} = 180,000 - 120,000
$$

### 3. Final Answer
- **Answer:** 
  - Net financial outcome for Alternative A: **$50,000**
  - Net financial outcome for Alternative B: **$60,000**
- **Check:** Both outcomes are positive, indicating that both alternatives are financially viable, with Alternative B being the more favorable option.
